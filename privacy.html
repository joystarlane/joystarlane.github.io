1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Starlane (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit our website, play our games, or contact us. It applies to Starlane services that link to this policy. Features and third-party services vary by game, platform, region and your choices; a game-specific notice takes priority where it provides more specific information.

For privacy questions and requests, contact zucomiceb522@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Gameplay and preferences: game progress, levels, scores, achievements, virtual items, settings, and interactions such as screen visits, button taps and ad rewards. Some progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Device and diagnostics: app and operating-system version, device model, memory information, language, approximate region derived from network information, IP address, app-instance or installation identifiers, crash reports, error logs and performance information.
  • Advertising: advertising identifiers where available and permitted, ad impressions, clicks, ad format, placement, rewards and revenue information. Advertising partners may process device and network information to serve, measure and protect advertising.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, purchase status and transaction or receipt information needed to validate purchases and restore eligible entitlements. Payments are processed by the app store; we do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Game services: if you use Google Play Games or a similar platform service, player identifiers, profile information made available by that service, sign-in status and achievement progress may be processed to provide those features.
  • Messages: your email address and the information you choose to send when contacting us.

Our website does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Its flower-color interaction runs locally in your browser. The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate our games, save progress, deliver purchases and rewards, support sign-in and achievements, respond to requests, diagnose crashes, improve gameplay and reliability, measure advertising and game engagement, prevent fraud and comply with applicable obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, support our legitimate interests in maintaining and improving our services, meet legal obligations, or follow your consent. Where consent is required for advertising or related processing, we request it through the relevant consent controls. You may withdraw consent through the controls provided in the game or by contacting us.

4. Service providers and advertising partners

Depending on the game, information may be processed by Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config), Google AdMob, Google Play Games and the app store. Advertising mediation may also use Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, ironSource, InMobi, Chartboost and Pangle. A partner being included in a game does not mean every partner receives data on every session.

These providers process information to deliver their services and, where applicable, for purposes described in their own privacy notices. Advertising partners may use identifiers and similar technologies for ad personalization, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to applicable consent and device settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, Google’s use of information from partner apps, Firebase privacy information, Unity, Liftoff, Meta, ironSource, InMobi, Chartboost and Pangle.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

5. Your choices and rights

You can manage notification permissions and advertising preferences in your device settings. Where available, use the game’s privacy or consent options to review your advertising choices. You can manage Google Play Games connections through your Google account. Disabling personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or the processing needed to deliver and measure them.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of targeted advertising or sharing as defined by local law. Contact us to exercise a right or ask about available controls. We may need reasonable information to verify your request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Please include the game name, platform and relevant player or installation identifier if available. Do not send passwords or full payment details. Uninstalling a game may delete local progress, but does not by itself delete information already held by service providers.

6. Retention, security and transfers

We retain information only as needed for the purposes described here, including operating the service, resolving issues, preventing fraud and meeting legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information and the services used; third-party services apply their own retention settings and policies. Local game data can usually be removed through your device’s app-storage controls.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, international transfers are subject to applicable safeguards.

7. Children’s privacy

Age suitability and availability depend on the individual game and its store listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us with the game name and relevant details so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
